What We Can Help With

We provide free legal advice and representation in the following areas. If you need help with something not listed, call us at 416-461-8102.

Ontario Works & ODSP

Questions about Ontario Works or Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) benefits? We can advise you on your rights and help with appeals.

Landlord & Tenant Issues

Having problems with your landlord or rental home? We can advise you on your rights as a tenant, lease agreements, eviction, repairs, and more.

Immigration

Need help understanding Canadian immigration laws or your status? We can provide legal advice on immigration matters.

Employment Insurance & Employment Law

Trouble with Employment Insurance (EI)? Questions about your rights at work? We can advise you on employment law and EI benefits.

Canada Pension Plan (CPP)

Questions about your Canada Pension Plan benefits or CPP disability? We can provide legal advice on CPP matters.

Criminal Injuries Compensation

Advice only. If you have been injured by a crime, we can advise you on compensation options available to you.

Family Violence

Experiencing family violence? We can advise you on restraining orders, protection orders, family law rights, and other options available to you.

Consumer Law

Been scammed or sold faulty goods? Have a dispute with a merchant or creditor? We can advise you on your consumer rights.

Small Claims Court

Advice only. Have a dispute involving money owed to you? We can advise you about taking a case to Small Claims Court.

Certified True Copies & Affidavits

Need a document certified as a true copy or signed as an affidavit? We offer this service in limited areas of law. Call for details.

Special Clinics & Services

Chinese-Speaking Legal Clinic

We run a clinic for Chinese-speaking clients every Tuesday afternoon from 1:30 pm to 4:00 pm. No appointment needed — just come in or call 416-461-8102.

Multilingual Support

We can arrange interpretation in many languages. Call us at 416-461-8102 to ask about interpretation services for your language.

More Than Legal Advice

Free Legal Workshops

We hold free public legal education seminars and workshops on topics important to our community. Topics cover benefits, housing, employment, family law, and more. Call us for information about upcoming workshops.

Multilingual Pamphlets

We have free legal information pamphlets available in many languages. These cover your legal rights in areas like housing, benefits, employment, and family law.

Law Reform & Test Cases

We participate in law reform initiatives and test case litigation to change laws and improve legal rights for people in our community.

Community Development & Tenant Organizing

We help community groups and tenant organizations get stronger and have a voice in decisions that affect them.
